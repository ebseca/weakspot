Weakspot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

The short version

Weakspot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network requests. The app does not even request permission to use the internet. Everything it records stays on your device.

Weakspot is open source. You do not have to take any of this on trust — the full source is published at github.com/ebseca/weakspot under the GNU General Public License v3.0, and every claim below can be checked against it.

What the app stores

Weakspot saves the following to your device's local app storage so your progress survives closing the app:

  • The flashcard libraries you have, including any you created yourself
  • Your recent answers for each card, which is how the app works out what you are weakest at
  • Which cards you have unlocked and which have been introduced to you
  • Your settings: deck language, number of answer options, cards in play, haptics, and whether Weakspot Pro is switched on

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone, because there are no servers and no third parties involved.

Making your own decks

Weakspot has no AI built in. To make a deck, the app writes a prompt that you copy to your clipboard and paste into whichever AI service you already use. You then paste that service's reply back into the app, or import it from a file you choose.

Weakspot itself sends nothing anywhere during this. What you paste into a third-party AI service is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not this one. The app reads only the text you paste or the single file you pick.

Weakspot Pro

Weakspot Pro is free. Switching it on is stored locally on your device. No payment is taken, no payment details are requested, and nothing is sent to any payment provider.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier
  • It does not track your location
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • It does not read your files, except a single file you explicitly choose to import a deck from
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • It does not make any network connections

Permissions

Weakspot requests no Android permissions that need your approval.

Children

Weakspot collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently removes everything it has stored. You can also clear it at any time via Android's Settings → Apps → Weakspot → Storage → Clear data, or delete a single library you made by long-pressing it in the app. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server-side copy to request the deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or introduces any network feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The change history is public in the repository's commit log.

Contact

Questions about this policy are best raised as an issue on the public repository, so the answer is visible to everyone: github.com/ebseca/weakspot/issues
